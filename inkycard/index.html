From words to flashcards.

Look up words, explore definitions, and save what you want to learn. InkyCard brings Yomitan dictionaries and Anki card creation to a native macOS app.

For macOS 26 and later.

Your dictionaries, together

Import dictionaries in Yomitan format, choose which ones to use, and arrange their order. Look up words and inflected forms, with definitions, dictionary images, word frequency, and pitch accents where your dictionaries provide them.

Make cards in Anki

Connect to Anki through AnkiConnect, choose your deck and note type, and map dictionary content to your existing fields. Add definitions and images, with optional pronunciation audio. Anki integration requires desktop Anki with the AnkiConnect add-on installed and running.

Listen and make it your own

Listen to pronunciation using the voices available on your Mac. Customize dictionary display with CSS and preview your changes inside the app.
